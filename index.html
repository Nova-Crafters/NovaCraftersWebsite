Free websites for nonprofits and small businesses.

NovaCrafters is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit started by a group of high school friends. We'll design and build your website, get it online, and show you how to update it yourself. It won't cost you anything.

What we do

Who
Nonprofits, whether you're officially registered or just getting started, and small businesses, especially new ones or ones working with a tight budget.
What
The website itself: the pages your visitors see and click through. We don't build behind-the-scenes systems like user accounts or databases.
Cost
Nothing. You won't pay for any part of it.
After launch
We'll show you how to make updates on your own, and we're here to help if something breaks or you need changes.

How it works

Here's what happens, from your first message to after your site is live.

  1. 01

    Reach out

    Fill out the form on our Contact page or email us at service@nova-crafters.com. Tell us a bit about your organization and what you'd like your website to do.

  2. 02

    Free consultation

    We'll set up a call to talk about which pages you need, how you want the site to look, and what it should do.

  3. 03

    Design and build

    We design and build your site. You can share feedback and ask for changes as we go.

  4. 04

    Review

    Once the design is ready, we walk you through it and fine-tune the details until you're happy with it.

  5. 05

    Launch and training

    We put your site online and show you how to update it yourself.

  6. 06

    Help after launch

    If something stops working, or you need an update or a new feature, just reach out and we'll help.

Questions

What does NovaCrafters do?+

We build websites for nonprofits and small businesses, for free. That way, not having a budget doesn't mean going without a website.

Who started NovaCrafters?+

A group of high school friends who love building websites and wanted to put that to use helping their community.

Why is it free?+

For us, this is community service. We want small organizations to be able to reach more people online, and we don't want cost to stand in the way.

Who can ask for a website?+

Nonprofits, whether you're a registered 501(c)(3) or an informal group, and small businesses, especially new ones or ones working with a tight budget.

What do you build?+

The website itself: the pages your visitors see and read. We design the pages, build them, put the site online, and show you how to update it.

What won't you build?+

Behind-the-scenes systems like user accounts or databases. If your site needs one of those, we won't be able to build that part.

What does it cost?+

Nothing. It's free.

How do I request a website?+

Fill out the form on our Contact page or email us at service@nova-crafters.com. We'll get back to you to set up a free consultation.

Do you help after the site launches?+

Yes. If you run into a problem, or need an update or a new feature, just reach out and we'll help.

How are you different from other web designers?+

We're a nonprofit, we don't charge, and we only work with nonprofits and small businesses.

Tell us a bit about your organization and what you need, and we'll get back to you to set up a free consultation.
