What we do

Who
Nonprofits, formal or informal, and small businesses, including new and under-resourced ones.
What
Frontend websites: the pages people see and read. We don't build backend systems such as user accounts or databases.
Cost
Free. We don't charge for any part of the work.
After launch
We show you how to update the site yourself, and we help with technical problems and updates when you need it.

How it works

Six steps, from your first message to after launch.

  1. 01

    Contact us

    Fill out the form on our Contact page or email service@nova-crafters.com. Tell us what your organization does and what you want the website to do.

  2. 02

    Free consultation

    We schedule a call to talk through what pages you need, how the site should look, and what it needs to do.

  3. 03

    Design and build

    We design and build your site. You can give feedback and ask for changes along the way.

  4. 04

    Review and approval

    We show you the finished design. We adjust the details with you until you approve it.

  5. 05

    Launch and training

    We launch the site and show you how to update it yourself.

  6. 06

    Help after launch

    If something stops working, or you need an update or a new feature, contact us and we'll help.

Questions

What does NovaCrafters do?+

We design and build websites for nonprofits and small businesses, for free, so they can have a working website without paying for one.

Who started NovaCrafters?+

A group of friends in high school who like building websites and wanted to use that skill for community service.

Why is it free?+

We started NovaCrafters as community service. We want small organizations to be able to reach more people online, and we don't want cost to be the reason they can't.

Who can ask for a website?+

Nonprofits, whether they are registered 501(c)(3) organizations or informal groups, and small businesses, including new and under-resourced ones.

What do you build?+

Frontend websites: the pages your visitors see and read. We design the pages, build them, launch the site, and show you how to update it.

What won't you build?+

Backend systems, such as user accounts or databases. If your site needs one of these, we can't build that part.

What does it cost?+

Nothing. The service is free.

How do I request a website?+

Fill out the form on our Contact page or email service@nova-crafters.com. We'll reply to schedule a free consultation about what you need.

Do you help after the site launches?+

Yes. If you run into a technical problem, or need an update or a new feature, contact us and we'll help.

How are you different from other web designers?+

We are a nonprofit, we don't charge, and we work only with nonprofits and small businesses.

Want a website for your organization?

Tell us what your organization does and what you need. We'll reply to set up a free consultation.
