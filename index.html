02 / Process

How it works

Here's what happens, from your first message to after your site is live.

  1. Step 01

    Reach out

    Fill out the form on our Contact page or email us at service@nova-crafters.com. Tell us a bit about your organization and what you'd like your website to do.

  2. Step 02

    Free consultation

    We'll set up a call to talk about which pages you need, how you want the site to look, and what it should do.

  3. Step 03

    Design and build

    We design and build your site. You can share feedback and ask for changes as we go.

  4. Step 04

    Review

    Once the design is ready, we walk you through it and fine-tune the details until you're happy with it.

  5. Step 05

    Launch and training

    We put your site online and show you how to update it yourself.

  6. Step 06

    Help after launch

    If something stops working, or you need an update or a new feature, just reach out and we'll help.

03 / FAQ

Questions

Still wondering about something? Email us at service@nova-crafters.com.

What does NovaCrafters do?+

We build websites for certified nonprofits, for free. That way, not having a budget doesn't mean going without a website. So far we've helped 180+ certified nonprofits in 4 countries, saving them about $730,000 in total.

Who started NovaCrafters?+

A group of high school friends who love building websites and wanted to put that to use helping their community.

Why is it free?+

For us, this is community service. We want nonprofits to be able to reach more people online, and we don't want cost to stand in the way.

Who can ask for a website?+

Certified nonprofits, especially new ones or ones working with a tight budget. We don't build websites for businesses.

Can nonprofits outside the U.S. apply?+

Yes. We've helped certified nonprofits in 4 countries so far.

What do you build?+

The website itself: the pages your visitors see and read. We design the pages, build them, put the site online, and show you how to update it.

What won't you build?+

Behind-the-scenes systems like user accounts or databases. If your site needs one of those, we won't be able to build that part.

What does it cost?+

Nothing. It's free.

How do I request a website?+

Fill out the form on our Contact page or email us at service@nova-crafters.com. We'll get back to you to set up a free consultation.

Do you help after the site launches?+

Yes. If you run into a problem, or need an update or a new feature, just reach out and we'll help.

How are you different from other web designers?+

We design a custom website for your nonprofit and build it for you, so you're not stuck assembling a do-it-yourself template. We also train your team to update it and keep helping after launch. And because we're a nonprofit ourselves, all of it is free.
