About us

A team of 10, building for nonprofits.

NovaCrafters is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. It started with two high school friends. Today we're a team of 10 who design, build, and update websites for certified nonprofits, and we don't charge for it.

Who we are

The team

From 2 friends to a team of 10

NovaCrafters started with two high school friends who love design and web development and wanted to use those skills to help their community. Today we're a team of 10.

Why it's free

Cost shouldn't be the barrier

A good website helps a nonprofit reach more people. We don't want cost to be the thing that keeps a nonprofit from having one.

How we work

With you, start to finish

We start with a free consultation to learn what you need. Then we design and build your site, take your feedback along the way, put it online, and show you how to update it yourself.
