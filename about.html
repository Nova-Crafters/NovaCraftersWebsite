We want you to be seen.

NovaCrafters is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. We design, build, and update websites for nonprofits and small businesses, and we don't charge for it.

Who we are

We're a group of friends who started NovaCrafters in high school. We love design and web development, and we wanted to use those skills to help our community.

Why we don't charge

A good website helps a nonprofit or small business reach more people. We don't want cost to be the thing that keeps a small organization from having one.

How we work

We start with a free consultation to learn what you need. Then we design and build your site, take your feedback along the way, put it online, and show you how to update it yourself.

The details

Who
Nonprofits, whether you're officially registered or just getting started, and small businesses, especially new ones or ones working with a tight budget. You don't need to be a registered 501(c)(3) to reach out.
What
The website itself: the pages people see and read. We design it, build it, put it online, and show you how to update it.
Not included
Behind-the-scenes systems like user accounts or databases.
After launch
We're around to help with problems and updates.
