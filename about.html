We want you to be seen.

NovaCrafters is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. We design, build, and update websites for nonprofits and small businesses, free of charge.

Who we are

We are a group of friends who started NovaCrafters in high school. We like design and web development, and we wanted to use those skills for community service.

Why we don't charge

We think a clear website helps a nonprofit or small business reach more people. We don't charge, so that cost isn't what keeps a small organization from having one.

Who we work with

Nonprofits, formal or informal, and small businesses, including new and under-resourced ones. You don't need to be a registered 501(c)(3) to ask.

What we build

Frontend websites: the pages people see and read. We design the site, build it, launch it, and show you how to update it. After launch, we help with technical problems and updates.

We don't build backend systems such as user accounts or databases.
